# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS. No build step; the site must be hostable on any static host.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the owner as a software developer. Their job: judge fit for a role in a short visit, then either contact the owner or download a CV. Visits are likely short and may happen on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio presenting the owner's software projects. Success means a recruiter understands what the owner builds, sees credible proof of it, and knows how to get in touch or where to find the CV.

## Positioning

Open decision: no distinguishing mechanism, specialization, or claim has been confirmed yet. Future work must not invent one. Ask before adding positioning copy.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a link (CV, LinkedIn, application, message) and typically evaluate on desktop or mobile. No internal workflow or tools are defined by the product itself.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content: software/development projects (apps, code, case studies, technical write-ups).
- Static site only: no backend, no auth, no CMS, unless the owner later confirms otherwise.
- Open decisions: owner name and display name, which projects to feature, contact method, whether a downloadable CV exists, language(s) of the copy.

## Brand Commitments

None confirmed. No name, logo, palette, typography, or voice has been provided. Any visual identity is to be established later in new-work.

## Evidence on Hand

The project folder (`D:\Projetos\Portifolio`) was empty at init time: no project write-ups, screenshots, testimonials, metrics, press, or CV file exist yet. Do not fabricate projects, outcomes, testimonials, employers, or metrics; use placeholders clearly marked as such until real evidence is supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the owner's technical work the first thing a recruiter sees; the portfolio is evidence, not decoration.
- Every project shown should answer what it is, what the owner did, and what it demonstrates.
- Keep the path to contact or CV obvious from every page.
- Stay static, fast, and dependency-light so it loads on any device and host.
